# New Hire Onboarding Checklist

For a new member joining the operations team.

## Day 1
- [ ] Issue laptop and building access card
- [ ] Create email and shared-drive accounts
- [ ] Walk through the warehouse and dispatch flow

## Week 1
- [ ] Shadow a support agent on live tickets
- [ ] Read the refund and exchange policy
- [ ] Meet the courier coordination contact

## Week 2
- [ ] Own a small queue of tickets with review
- [ ] Complete data-handling and anonymisation training
