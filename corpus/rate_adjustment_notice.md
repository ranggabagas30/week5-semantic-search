# Notice: Service Cost Adjustment

Effective next quarter, the cost of our logistics service will
rise by 8%.

## Why
- Fuel is more expensive than a year ago.
- Courier partners have revised their fees upward.

## What it means for you
- Orders placed **this month** keep the current rate.
- New contracts from next quarter reflect the adjusted figure.

We kept the change as small as we could and will review it again
in six months.
